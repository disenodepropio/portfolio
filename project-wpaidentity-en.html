COMMUNICATION | BRANDING

Weplan Analytics

Period of time

2024

Duration

1 month

Weplan Analytics needed a visual identity that matched the complexity of its product: cutting-edge technology, technical excellence and a strong position against much larger competitors. Its previous identity did not convey these values or reflect the precision and innovation behind the brand.

Objectives

Create a new corporate identity that conveys:

  • Its advanced technology is based on machine learning and artificial intelligence algorithms.
  • Its competitive edge in the market lies in its quality and technical expertise.
  • Its role as a leader in innovation within the telecommunications sector.

Furthermore, there was one key requirement: the new logo had to maintain a visual link with the previous identity, incorporating a conceptual element related to telecommunications and networks.

1. Exploring the concept of branding

I analysed the brand’s core values and defined the key attributes of its identity:


Innovation → Precision → Technology → Trust.

2. Evolution of the logo

I redesigned the logo, retaining a subtle nod to the original version whilst updating it with a cleaner, more geometric and technical aesthetic.


A symbol inspired by the concept of networks and connections was incorporated, representing the software’s ability to transform complex data into clear decisions.

3. Comprehensive visual system

Once the logo had been finalised, I created a scalable brand identity:

  • A colour palette inspired by technology.
  • A modern and highly legible typeface.
  • A modular graphical system for representing data and connectivity.

4. Brand application

The new brand identity was rolled out across:

  • Website.
  • Documentation and commercial proposals.
  • Social media and corporate materials.

Previously, the brand lacked visual consistency. Now, everything is unified under a robust corporate style guide, making Weplan Analytics instantly recognisable across all platforms.

The result

A modern, tech-driven and distinctive visual identity, in keeping with the company’s high standards of excellence.


The project took around a month to complete, and since then the brand has been gradually transitioning all its materials to this new identity.
