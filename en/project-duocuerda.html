Branding

Marta & Victoria

Visual identity for a string duo: logotype, visual system and Instagram presence designed from scratch for a violin and cello duo.

Project objective

  • ○Create an identity that conveyed elegance without feeling distant or overly formal.
  • ○Build a recognisable brand to position themselves in the events market.
  • ○Define a coherent visual system applicable across all digital touchpoints.
  • ○Launch an Instagram presence aligned with the identity from day one.
Type Personal project
Disciplines Branding · Social
Sector Music · Events

The starting point

Marta and Victoria have been playing together for years: violin and cello, two instruments that complement each other naturally. The challenge was to turn that musical connection into a brand that could open doors in the events world — weddings, celebrations, corporate acts.

We started from scratch: no established name, no image, no digital presence. The project covered everything from defining the brand concept to launching their Instagram profile.

An identity that sounded as good as their music: elegant, approachable and with a personality of its own.

Logotype and identity

The logotype stems from the concept of duality: two instruments, two personalities, one voice. The typographic composition combines a letterform with classical character and a clean, contemporary structure — striking the balance between musical tradition and modernity.

The palette draws on warm, refined tones that evoke the wood of string instruments, without falling into classical-music clichés. The result is an identity that feels genuine and approachable.

  • Primary logotype and secondary versions.
  • Colour palette based on warm and neutral tones.
  • Typographic system with clear hierarchy.
  • Usage guide for digital applications.

Visual system

A coherent brand across every touchpoint.

Beyond the logo, the visual system defines how the brand behaves in any context: which typefaces to use, how to combine colours, the tone of imagery and how compositions are structured.

Colour. Warm tones that evoke warmth and distinction without feeling heavy.

Typography. A typeface with classical personality paired with a clean sans-serif for supporting text.

Visual style. Warm-toned photography and compositions with air and breathing room.

Tone of voice. Approachable and elegant: real music for moments that matter.

Instagram presence

With the identity in place, the next step was building their digital presence from the very first post. I designed the content strategy and visual templates so the feed would work as a cohesive whole: every post reinforces the brand and communicates who they are View Instagram ↗.

The goal wasn't just a beautiful profile — it was ensuring that anyone visiting would understand in seconds what the duo offers, what kind of events they cover, and why they should book them.

  • Reusable, consistent post templates.
  • Bio, highlights and initial content strategy.
  • Defined photographic and editing style.
  • First publications designed and ready to post.
