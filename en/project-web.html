UX/UI Project

Web

Complete redesign of the corporate website: new architecture, brand narrative, and visual experience for a SaaS company that transforms complex data into clear decisions in the telecom sector.

Project goal

  • ○Refresh the digital identity to reflect the real value of the software.
  • ○Redefine the architecture and narrative for immediate understanding.
  • ○Align the design with the new corporate identity.
  • ○Improve retention, navigation, and conversion KPIs.
Period 2025
Duration 3 months
Link Visit website ↗

The design process

The existing website did not communicate who the company was. The visual design was outdated, the narrative lacked clarity, and interaction was minimal. The challenge was not just updating colors or typography: the entire digital experience had to be rethought from scratch.

01 — Benchmarking Analysis and starting point

I studied the existing website and competitors in the sector to identify friction points: lack of clarity in the value proposition, a rigid structure that failed to guide users, and a visual style that did not convey innovation. This diagnosis shaped the entire strategy moving forward.

Benchmarking Heuristic analysis
02 — Strategy Architecture and narrative

I designed a content architecture that works as a natural journey: explaining what the company does, visually showing how the software works, and guiding the user toward contact or a demo. I also defined all website copy, from headlines to interaction microcopy.

UX Writing Copywriting
03 — Design UX/UI and visual system

From structural wireframes to final mockups: information hierarchy, navigation, and interaction points aligned with the new corporate identity. Colors, typography, and modular sections designed for quick and frictionless reading.

Design that reaches production

I worked closely with the development team to ensure the implementation maintained the same fluidity as the prototypes, and with the business team so the narrative maintained a consistent focus.

Good design does not end with the prototype: it ends when the user experiences it.

The result

A website that communicates, converts, and represents.

The redesign was not just a visual update: it transformed the website into a strategic communication channel. More visual, more concise, and fully aligned with the brand identity.

Clear value proposition in less than 5 seconds.
Higher retention and interaction on key pages of the website.
Perfect responsive design across all devices.
A modern corporate image that conveys trust and innovation.
