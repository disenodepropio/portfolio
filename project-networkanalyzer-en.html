UX UI DESIGN | WEB & MOBILE APP

Network Analyzer open_in_new

Period of time

2025

Duration

3 months

In 2024, Weplan’s Network Analyzer was developed, aimed at technical professionals. The app enables the analysis of mobile and Wi-Fi networks in real time, displaying performance metrics, KPIs and complex data in a professional and accessible way. The aim was to provide an accurate and reliable tool, with a digital experience aligned with the brand’s technical and professional identity.

10K+
Downloads
4.4 ★
Rating

Project objective

  • Design a powerful and intuitive Android app from scratch for technical users.
  • Create the Network Analyzer sub-brand identity, consistent with the parent brand and emphasising professionalism and precision.
  • Design the landing page to explain the product and maximise downloads.
  • Develop strategic visual content for the Google Play Store, Google Ads campaigns and social media promotion.

1. UX/UI of the app open_in_new

The app was designed with a comprehensive UX/UI approach:

  • Information architecture and user flows, enabling clear and efficient access to complex features.
  • Wireframes and interactive prototypes, validated with technical users.
  • A comprehensive visual design, with a professional, technical style that is consistent and easy to read.
  • A reusable component-based system, designed to ensure scalability and consistency in future updates.

The result is a robust, user-friendly and professional app, tailored to the needs of discerning users.

2. Website and digital presence open_in_new

A landing page was designed to explain the product and encourage downloads:

  • A clear and straightforward structure, highlighting key features and benefits.
  • Visual design consistent with the app’s identity and that of the parent brand.
  • Key elements for conversions: prominent buttons, calls to action and intuitive navigation.

3. Branding and promotion

The Network Analyzer brand identity conveys technical expertise, professionalism and reliability, whilst maintaining a connection with the parent brand. The digital assets were developed in line with these principles:

  • Strategic screenshots for the Google Play Store.
  • Ad creatives for Google Ads, tailored to a technical and professional tone.
  • Social media content, boosting the sub-brand’s visibility and recognition.

Impact

  • Weplan’s first app to gain significant traction in the market.
  • Over 10,000 downloads, with a 4.4-star rating and 226 reviews on the Google Play Store.
  • A comprehensive project encompassing UX/UI, branding, web design and promotion, delivering solid and widely recognised results.
