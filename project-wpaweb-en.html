UX UI DESIGN | WEB

WePlan Analytics open_in_new

Period of time

2025

Duration

3 months

WePlan Analytics, a company specialising in data analytics software, needed a website that would reflect its core value: facilitating strategic decision-making based on complex data. The project involved a complete redesign of the website, aligning it with the new corporate identity and creating a clear, visual and interactive experience that would enable users to quickly understand the software and navigate the site with ease.

How the project began

When I joined the project, the Weplan Analytics website did not reflect who the company really was. The design was outdated, the narrative unclear and interaction minimal. The biggest challenge was to revamp the site from scratch, aligning it with the new corporate identity and ensuring that any visitor could understand the value of the software within seconds. It wasn’t just a matter of updating the colours or typography: the entire digital experience needed to be rethought.

1. Benchmarking and analysis

The first step was to analyse both the existing website and industry benchmarks. This enabled me to identify:

  • Lack of clarity in the value proposition.
  • A rigid structure that did not guide the user.
  • An outdated design that failed to convey innovation or inspire confidence.

This analysis served as a starting point for redefining the structure and key messages.

2. Structure and narrative

Based on that analysis, I designed a new content architecture. The idea was for the website to function as a natural journey: explaining what the company does, visually demonstrating how its software works, and guiding the user towards the contact form or a demo. I also defined the website’s narrative and all the copy, from product descriptions to interactive microcopy. I focused on ensuring it was clear, concise and aligned with the new brand voice.

3. UX/UI design

The next step was to translate that narrative into a visual format. I began with a flowchart and structural wireframes, defining the information hierarchy, navigation and interaction points: buttons, menus and calls to action. I then designed the final mock-ups, in line with the new corporate identity:

  • Colours and typefaces consistent with the brand.
  • Modular sections designed for quick reading.
  • Clear buttons and interactions that guide the user seamlessly.

Collaboration and development

I worked closely with a developer to take the design into production. This enabled me to ensure that the implementation retained the same fluidity as the prototypes: from the responsive behaviour to the interactions with buttons and menus.

The result

The result was a completely revamped website:

  • More visual and concise, capable of explaining what the company does in a matter of seconds.
  • More interactive and user-friendly, improving retention and navigation KPIs.
  • More in line with the corporate identity, conveying a sense of modernity and trust.

The redesign not only improved the website’s appearance, but also turned it into a more effective and strategic communication channel for the company.
