GRAPHIC DESIGN | VIDEO

Corporate video open_in_new

Period of time

2024

Duration

3 days

In 2024, Weplan needed a corporate video for a key event, in which it had to present the company in a clear, compelling and highly professional manner. The challenge was to condense into just a few minutes who we are, what we do and what sets us apart.

Objectives

Create a video that conveys: The essence of Weplan as a company specialising in data analysis and advanced AI algorithms. Its unique position in the market: cutting-edge technology, precision, quality and technical excellence. A corporate tone that is direct and visually striking, suitable for a professional event.

Process

1. Defining the key message

Organising all the company’s key information to create a clear, concise and impact-focused narrative: who we are, what we do, and how we do it.

2. Script and visual narrative

Development of a clear and concise corporate script.

3. Visual direction

A design aesthetic in keeping with the brand identity: a corporate colour palette and typeface, and a visual rhythm that complements the narrative without overwhelming it.

4. Coordination and production

Editing, animation and final assembly, ensuring consistency with the brand and a smooth narrative flow throughout the video.

The result

A concise, clear and visually striking corporate video that summarises Weplan’s value proposition in just a few minutes. It was presented at an international event – the Mobile World Congress.
