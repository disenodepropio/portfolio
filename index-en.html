Where I come from, we say that something is done "de propio" when it’s done with a specific purpose in mind, with enthusiasm, and with careful thought.

This is how I see design: every decision, every colour, every word, every space and every structure has a reason behind it.

Designing is thinking, empathising, structuring. It’s about doing "de propio".
